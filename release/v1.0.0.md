# F.E.C.H.A. v1.0.0

**Turn strong work into movement.**

This is the first public, model-agnostic release of F.E.C.H.A.

F.E.C.H.A. is a practical decision and execution framework designed to prevent good work from staying open, vague, or unused.

It helps turn an idea, artifact, concept, prototype, proposal, message, meeting, or piece of work into one of three outcomes:

- a decision;
- a pilot or test;
- a clear next step with owner and timing.

## What is included

- canonical framework;
- canonical AI instructions;
- full F.E.C.H.A. template;
- quick-check template;
- ChatGPT guide;
- Gemini guide;
- generic AI conversation guide;
- Cursor guide;
- Codex guide;
- examples for design, creative work, AI workflows, and meeting closure.

## The sequence

- **Foco**
- **Enquadramento**
- **Caminho mínimo**
- **Handoff de decisão**
- **Amarração**

The original Portuguese names remain canonical.

## Core principle

> Do not let valuable work end with “What do you think?” when it should end with a recommendation, decision, pilot, or next step.
