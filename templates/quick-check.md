# F.E.C.H.A. Quick Check

Before important work moves forward, ask:

1. **Foco** — What decision or movement is needed?
2. **Enquadramento** — Why does it matter now?
3. **Caminho mínimo** — What is the smallest useful next move?
4. **Handoff de decisão** — What response is needed from others?
5. **Amarração** — What was decided, who owns the next step, and by when?

If one answer is missing, the work may still be too open.
